Do You Need an Emergency Root Canal?

An emergency root canal removes infected or damaged tissue from inside your tooth, stops the pain and protects the tooth from further harm. Waiting often makes the infection worse, so early care matters.

  • Severe or throbbing tooth pain
  • Lingering sensitivity to hot or cold
  • Swollen gums or facial swelling
  • A pimple-like bump on the gum
  • Deep crack or injury exposing the nerve

FAQs

Emergency Dentist Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about emergency root canals and urgent dental care.

When do I need an emergency root canal?

You may need an emergency root canal if you have severe, throbbing tooth pain, lingering sensitivity to hot or cold, swelling around the tooth or gums, a pimple-like bump on the gum, or a deep crack or injury that has exposed the nerve. These are signs of infection or inflamed pulp that should be examined right away.

Does an emergency root canal hurt?

The procedure is performed under local anesthesia, so most patients feel pressure rather than pain. The goal of an emergency root canal is to remove the source of your toothache and bring relief.

Can I get a same day dentist appointment in Jersey City?

Yes. We focus on urgent dental appointments and same day dental care. Call 551-529-5148 and we will arrange the earliest available emergency visit.

What should I do while waiting for my emergency dental visit?

Rinse gently with warm salt water, use a cold compress on the outside of the cheek for swelling, keep your head elevated and avoid very hot, cold or hard foods. Do not place aspirin directly on the gum. If you have facial swelling with fever or trouble breathing or swallowing, go to the nearest emergency room.

Do you offer affordable emergency dental care?

We aim to make emergency dental care accessible. Contact us to discuss your treatment, costs and payment options before your visit.
